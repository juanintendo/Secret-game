## How to use this document

This document separates four kinds of knowledge:

- **Settled authorial canon:** true in the project even if the player does not know it yet.
- **Player-facing knowledge:** what the player or protagonists can reasonably know at a given point.
- **Provisional design:** strong current direction that can still change.
- **Open question:** intentionally unresolved by the creative team.

When this file conflicts with narrative material in `90S-ANIME-XCOM-PREPRODUCTION-001.md`, this file governs story canon. The preproduction document continues to govern technical, visual, combat, and production direction unless explicitly superseded.

## 1. Core dramatic premise

The story follows the destruction and eventual reformation of an elite three-woman tactical unit.

The original unit operated with extraordinary coordination under a human field leader and a remote female Operator. A targeted act of payback destroyed the unit during the tutorial mission. Two members survived and disappeared underground. Years later, Operator—also presumed destroyed—finds them separately and begins maneuvering them into a new team with one specific human recruit.

The definitive trio has no appointed leader. Their character and progression arc is the transformation from three powerful, mistrustful competitors into a unit capable of surpassing the original team through earned synchronization.

## 2. The original team

### 2.1 Original human leader

Status: settled authorial canon except where marked open.

- She is a fully human woman.
- Fully human does not mean low-tech.
- She uses an independent AI copilot in real time for threat analysis, tactical simulation, probability, targeting, and decision support.
- She is the established field leader of the original trio.
- She leads through judgment and responsibility, not merely superior combat statistics.
- She can recognize when an AI recommendation is computationally defensible but contextually, strategically, or morally unacceptable.
- She can override that recommendation and personally accept the cost.
- Her local AI copilot is distinct from Operator.
- Her AI advises; Operator coordinates; the leader retains final field authority.
- She has a specific personal relationship with each original teammate, not a generic commander/subordinate relationship.

Open:

- Name, age, background, and exact combat class.
- Her current canonical appearance is settled: green hair, removable external white/gray AI-assisted armor, and a short fitted red bomber jacket.
- Whether she has implants, wearables, neural interfaces, or other augmentative technology.
- The precise limitations and personality of her AI copilot.
- Her relationship history with Operator.
- What she ultimately became after the tutorial disaster.

### 2.2 Cyborg mech pilot

Status: settled authorial canon except where marked open.

- Her current canonical appearance uses long layered blonde hair, mechanical forearms/hands, and practical blue-gray field clothing. The earlier dark-haired reference is no longer current hair canon.
- She is a cyborg, not a full synthetic.
- She is the only principal character with a mech.
- Her tactical spectrum is tank, disruption/crowd control, breach, interception, and heavy DPS.
- Her primary attribute axis is Strength.
- Her current Specialty directions are: classic tank/support through protection; remote mech operation while she uses heavy ranged weapons; and a mech stance-dance build spanning committed melee damage, bleed/rupture, disengage, repositioning, and limited earned recovery.
- She survives the tutorial disaster because the original leader creates an escape path.
- She spends the following years underground without certainty that the enemy has stopped hunting her.
- Her body and mech create unique problems for hiding, maintenance, detection, and trust.

Open:

- Exact biological/technological composition.
- How much she can operate without the mech.
- How she interprets the leader's sacrifice.
- Whether she blames herself, the synthetic, Operator, or the entire system.

### 2.3 Luminous synthetic

Status: settled authorial canon except where marked open.

- She is the black-haired woman with the luminous internal skeleton from reference 05.
- She is fully synthetic.
- Her luminous skeleton/internal structure is literal character design, not merely an X-ray metaphor placed over a human.
- She survives the tutorial disaster because the original leader creates an escape path.
- She spends the following years underground without certainty that the enemy has stopped hunting her.
- The original intrusion creates a specific psychological and technical trauma around agency, internal systems, and remote access.
- Her primary attribute axis is Intellect and her tactical verb direction is Rewrite / Conduct.
- Her current Specialty directions are: Heartless invasive lightning damage; hacking/biohacking crowd control; and conditional spell-slinger support inspired by the original leader.
- In the definitive-team timeline, she wears the original human leader's short fitted red bomber jacket.

Open:

- Exact balance and implementation of her established Intellect/Rewrite/Conduct identity.
- Exact construction and origin.
- How she distinguishes thought, instruction, memory, and unauthorized influence.
- How she interprets the leader's sacrifice.

### 2.4 Operator

Status: settled authorial canon except where marked open.

- Operator is female-presenting and remotely coordinates briefings, intelligence, and tactical support.
- Her presence is disembodied, controlled, observant, dry, and psychologically imposing.
- “GLaDOS-like” defines her dramatic function and pressure, not an imitation of GLaDOS's dialogue or comedy.
- Operator and the original leader's local AI copilot are different entities.
- During the original-team era, Operator is physically present and alive at hidden HQ rather than appearing only on monitors.
- Her canonical original-era appearance uses enormous ethereal white hair, white clothing with purple details, a right arm bare below the shoulder armor and ungloved, and a left arm sleeved/gloved.
- Operator operates through a hidden headquarters and distributed/compartmentalized infrastructure.
- She continuously backs up information required for some form of restoration or continued existence.
- She appears to be eliminated during the tutorial attack on HQ.
- Years later, she exists again and executes a plan to locate and reunite the two survivors.
- In the current definitive-team visual canon, she manifests to the field team as a coherent human-shaped hologram rather than a physically present woman.
- Her recontact with each survivor is individually designed around that woman's history, hiding strategy, fears, and security posture.
- Reforming the team is part of Operator's plan, not a sentimental reunion or casual request.

Open:

- Whether Operator began as a human, AI, upload, hybrid, or something else.
- Whether restored Operator is subjectively continuous with the one apparently killed.
- Timestamp and completeness of her surviving state.
- What memory, authority, embodiment, or access she lost.
- Whether she was altered, forked, released, or unknowingly compromised.
- Her complete plan and why she cannot reveal it.

## 3. The definitive team

### 3.1 White-haired human recruit

Status: settled authorial canon except where marked open.

- She is the white/silver-haired woman from reference 01.
- She is the only fully human member of the definitive trio.
- She is not the original leader and must not become a cosmetic recreation of her.
- She is recruited years after the tutorial.
- Operator requires her specifically; an interchangeable capable operative will not satisfy the plan.
- The true reason for requiring her is not revealed immediately.
- Personality direction: elusive, irreverent, sensually confident, self-interested, improvisational, and difficult to contain.
- Touchstone energy: Faye Valentine, without copying her biography or voice.
- Physical direction: fluid agility, timing, improvisation, and looseness reminiscent of Spike Spiegel.
- Tactical territory: mobility, feints, seduction/manipulation, flanking, evasion, and opportunistic precision.
- Her primary attribute axis is Agility.
- Her current Specialty directions are Dispatch (agile melee assassination), Con Girl (Charisma-driven manipulation/crowd control at a DPS tradeoff), and Gunslinger (mobile ranged execution).
- Current definitive-team art uses short/tousled pale white-lavender hair and a clean black tactical outfit without fur, feathers, or production-hostile trim.
- She resists being treated as the missing leader's replacement.

Open:

- Why she is uniquely required.
- Name, history, loyalties, debts, and existing connection to the conspiracy.
- Whether she knows more about the old attack than she initially admits.

### 3.2 No new leader

Status: settled authorial canon.

- None of the definitive trio is appointed permanent leader.
- The absence of hierarchy is an intentional psychological and mechanical condition.
- The two survivors initially try, consciously or unconsciously, to rebuild the geometry of the old team.
- The white-haired recruit rejects the empty role they project onto her.
- The new unit only succeeds when all three stop attempting to recreate the missing woman.
- The recruit is not level one; the unit is level one.

### 3.3 Cohesion arc

Status: settled direction; precise mechanics provisional.

The unit progresses along two axes:

- individual capability;
- collective synchronization.

At first, the protagonists optimize their own turns, compete for control, waste openings, and lack reliable handoffs. As trust develops, they unlock assists, rescues, initiative exchanges, chained movement, combination attacks, and synchronized limit breaks.

The shared resource grows primarily through real team play rather than ordinary damage accumulation.

## 4. Hidden headquarters

Status: settled authorial canon.

- HQ has a physical and/or infrastructural location unknown to the field team.
- Nobody on the original field team knows its location, including the human leader.
- Communications pass through blinded and compartmentalized relays.
- The antagonist did not discover HQ by simply following the leader.
- The successful attack implies penetration of Operator's infrastructure, relay topology, synchronization architecture, an internal trust boundary, or a combination of them.
- HQ and the tutorial mission site are distinct locations attacked as parts of the same coordinated operation.

Open:

- Exact nature and location of HQ.
- Whether HQ is singular, distributed, mobile, virtualized, or intentionally misrepresented even to Operator.
- Exact attack vector.

## 5. Tutorial mission chronology

Status: settled sequence; exact mission fiction open.

### 5.1 Power fantasy

1. The original trio begins near maximum combat capability.
2. The team exhibits practiced, almost effortless synchronization.
3. The player learns selected advanced mechanics without seeing an entire endgame-sized ability tree.
4. Team actions fill a shared synchronization/limit resource.
5. The first combined limit break succeeds spectacularly.
6. The apparent mission approaches a clean conclusion.

### 5.2 Prepared intrusion

7. The team prepares and commits to a second synchronized limit break.
8. The synchronization event exposes a privileged connection between cyborg, synthetic, mech, tactical systems, Operator, and/or the distributed command infrastructure.
9. A hostile intrusion prepared in advance uses that moment of maximum connection.
10. The cyborg and synthetic suffer simultaneous system failure or loss of agency.
11. The field team realizes the apparent mission was bait within a larger act of payback.
12. Hidden HQ is attacked at the same time.

### 5.3 Live collapse

13. Corrupted communications leak gunfire, alarms, resistance, and a confrontation at HQ.
14. Operator appears to be eliminated live.
15. The field battlefield collapses into misinformation, disabled systems, broken coordination, and enemy pressure.
16. The original leader's AI copilot calculates or recommends the machine-optimal response.
17. The leader judges that response unacceptable and overrides it.
18. Her human judgment creates the escape path the antagonist failed to model.
19. She stays behind and protects/extracts the cyborg and synthetic.
20. The two survivors escape immediately before the mission site is destroyed.
21. No definitive body or proof of the leader's death survives.
22. HQ also falls silent.

### 5.4 Immediate interpretation

What the survivors/player believe:

- Operator was killed at HQ.
- The original leader almost certainly died when the field site was destroyed.
- The attack was targeted payback rather than an ordinary failed mission.
- Surviving the extraction does not mean the two remaining operatives are safe.

What is authorially true:

- Operator continues or is restored in some form.
- The original leader survives in some form and later becomes the unknown protective intruder.
- The exact state, embodiment, autonomy, motives, and cost of the leader's survival remain open.

## 6. Underground years

Status: settled authorial canon; personal details open.

- The cyborg and synthetic cannot verify that the threat ended.
- They separate.
- They erase or abandon old identities.
- They minimize network exposure and old-system usage.
- They do not maintain a normal relationship with each other.
- Their survival is prolonged uncertainty rather than peace.
- Years pass before Operator recontacts them.

Open:

- Duration of the gap.
- Where and how each lives.
- Whether either attempts contact and fails.
- What each sacrifices to remain hidden.

## 7. Operator's return and reassembly plan

Status: settled direction; exact sequences provisional.

- Operator contacts each survivor separately.
- The contacts are planned operations, not calls or exposition dumps.
- Operator proves enough intimate continuity to gain attention but cannot demand immediate trust.
- Each survivor gets a different security negotiation suited to her character.
- The two paths can converge on the same first mission without either survivor knowing the other was contacted.
- Their reunion can therefore feel both engineered and manipulative.
- The main timeline begins with cyborg and synthetic before the white-haired human joins.
- Operator then directs or maneuvers them toward the specific recruit required by her larger plan.

Provisional contact language:

- **Cyborg:** an external, physical, or mech-maintenance channel that avoids accessing her body directly.
- **Synthetic:** an analog, optical, acoustic, or air-gapped signal she can inspect voluntarily before accepting.

## 8. The unknown protective intruder

### 8.1 Authorial truth

Status: settled authorial canon.

- The unknown protector is the original human leader, surviving or continuing in a form not yet defined.
- She has quietly patched and reinforced the team's systems over an extended period.
- Operator initially does not know that these patches exist.
- The leader later assists the new team during a small number of specific high-value moments.
- Her behavior is inspired by Deepthroat's intrusion into operations in *Metal Gear Solid*: sparse, knowledgeable, ambiguous, and impossible to safely classify.
- She does not openly identify herself.
- She knows the team, its AI, its synchronization architecture, and its operational behavior from the inside.

### 8.2 First player-facing manifestation

Status: settled sequence; mission context open.

The first overt intervention occurs around the middle of the game.

1. The team encounters an attack with the same or closely related signature as the original tutorial collapse.
2. Visual and audio language deliberately triggers the survivors' trauma: corrupted UI, interrupted comms, contested agency, and apparent loss of control.
3. An unknown entity interrupts communications and/or directly takes control of the team AI's operational agency.
4. For a brief period, the team cannot know whether this is the original attacker repeating the trap.
5. Instead of weakening their systems, the intruder shields, stabilizes, or reroutes them.
6. The intruder guides them through that specific scenario and then disappears.
7. The intervention saves the unit but does not solve the mission for the player.

The first manifestation must feel like an inverted echo of the prologue: the same wound opens, but an unseen hand prevents the second catastrophe.

### 8.3 Immediate briefing

Status: settled story beat.

- After the mission, Operator confirms that the unit was exposed to the same attack family or mechanism.
- Operator also confirms that an unknown third party protected the system.
- At this moment, Operator does not know the protector's identity or that the protector has been modifying the architecture for a longer period.
- Operator quarantines the affected system and begins a private forensic investigation.
- The team is left with an unresolved possibility: protector, attacker, copy, trap, or competing entity.

### 8.4 Later forensic revelation

Status: settled story beat; exact timing open.

Operator eventually discovers that the emergency intervention was not the intruder's first contact with their systems.

- Someone has been installing small patches, repairs, hardening measures, or compensating changes without Operator noticing.
- The changes predate the overt rescue.
- They do not weaken, monitor, or sabotage the system in any obvious way.
- Individually, they resemble legitimate self-healing, redundancy recovery, compatibility fixes, or normal distributed maintenance.
- Operator's own compartmentalization prevents any single subsystem from seeing the complete historical pattern.
- Taken together, they have been strengthening it against attack.
- The post-intervention quarantine creates the first trustworthy frozen snapshot from which Operator can compare layers and reconstruct the sequence.
- The work demonstrates intimate knowledge of the original team and top-to-bottom understanding of the architecture.
- Operator reveals this finding to the team only after privately verifying it.

This revelation expands the mystery rather than resolving it: an entity capable of bypassing Operator undetected has apparently chosen to protect them.

### 8.5 Fair-play evidence

The protector mystery must leave evidence that supports the eventual leader reveal in retrospect:

- patches reflect the original leader's decision-making habits rather than merely her technical knowledge;
- the protector occasionally overrides an optimal action for the same human reason the leader demonstrated in the prologue;
- route choices or rescue priorities reproduce an old team pattern;
- the intruder knows details unavailable from stolen schematics alone;
- the intruder never asks the team to trust her identity.

Do not use an obvious catchphrase or signature immediately. Recognition should accumulate through behavior.

### 8.6 Intervention limits

To avoid a deus ex machina:

- interventions are rare;
- the protector creates survivable options rather than completing objectives;
- she cannot or will not remain connected;
- each intervention risks detection, destabilization, or revealing her to Operator and the original attacker;
- her access does not grant unlimited control over every system;
- the team must still choose and act.

Open:

- Whether the leader is physically alive.
- Whether she is embodied, uploaded, distributed, duplicated, partially reconstructed, or operating through stolen infrastructure.
- Why she does not reveal herself.
- Whether she can contact Operator directly.
- Whether she trusts the restored Operator.
- Why she began patching the new system and how early she gained access.
- What price she pays for each manifestation.
- When and how the player receives definitive confirmation of her identity.

## 10. Mystery architecture

Primary questions:

1. Who planned the original act of payback?
2. How was the hidden HQ penetrated?
3. What happened to the original leader?
4. What exactly returned as Operator?
5. Who is the protective intruder?
6. Why can the protector bypass Operator and understand every system layer?
7. Why was the white-haired human uniquely required?
8. What does Operator's plan actually intend to accomplish?

Constraint:

These questions should share causal machinery. They must not resolve as eight unrelated twists.

## 11. Thematic spine

Current themes emerging from settled canon:

- recommendation versus judgment;
- intelligence versus authority;
- survival versus continuity;
- trust after compromised agency;
- whether a restored person is the same person;
- leadership as responsibility for unacceptable choices;
- the difference between rebuilding a lost team and becoming a new one;
- protection that cannot safely identify itself.

## 12. Current story development order

Immediate next objective:

1. Define the original leader as a person before deciding what she became.
2. Define each survivor's underground life and recontact sequence.
3. Define Operator's restoration limits and private plan.
4. Define the apparent tutorial mission and the prior act that made the attack payback.
5. Define the white-haired recruit's unique necessity.
6. Only then decide the true attacker and the leader's post-destruction form.

This order preserves causality: the answers should emerge from character and system rules rather than being selected only for surprise value.
