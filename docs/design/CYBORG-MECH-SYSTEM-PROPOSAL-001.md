# Cyborg / Mech combat system proposal 001

**Status:** `RECOMMENDATION — NOT CANON`  
**Owner:** Juan decides product fantasy; Mina owns technical acceptance.  
**Purpose:** translate Juan's 2026-09-09 concept into a bounded system that can be falsified before content production.

## 1. Protected intent

The cyborg should feel like a pilot, brawler and mechanic rather than a conventional caster. Her body and mech remain useful both together and apart. The player manages commitment: board the mech, separate to cover more space, spend charge for force, or sacrifice pressure to recover it.

The player-facing commands should be **Board Mech** and **Deploy Remotely**. `Docked` remains the internal simulation state. Boarding means the cyborg remains an authoritative entity but becomes non-spatial, non-selectable, non-targetable and loses her initiative slot while the mech owns map occupancy.

The map uses square **cells**, not hexes.

## 2. One mech, three configurations

Juan's “three mechs” fantasy is retained as three visually strong configurations of the **same repaired machine**. A literal replacement per Specialty conflicts with established canon and would multiply modelling, rigging, animation, damage-state and cinematic continuity costs.

Every configuration must preserve the same:

- core chassis, cockpit and skeletal proportions;
- serial identity and recognizable head/torso language;
- accumulated repairs and signature damage;
- base rig, locomotion family and gameplay footprint policy.

| Specialty | Working configuration description | Tactical identity | Modular visual change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bulwark | Fortress configuration | Boarded defense, interception, control zones and protection | Reinforced armor, shield emitters and defensive hardpoints |
| Remote Arsenal | Synth-link configuration | Pilot and mech occupy separate positions; heavy range plus queued mech directives | Synthetic-derived control skin, actuators and ranged package; never a different biomechanical creature |
| Redline | Stripped prototype configuration | Boarded bruiser, efficient close combat, stance pressure and recovery | Energy-hungry modules removed to expose the original aggressive frame |

These are working descriptions, not final public names. Bulwark, Remote Arsenal and Redline remain the current Specialty names.

## 3. Shared Charge economy

Use one authoritative integer resource pool for the pilot/mech combat identity. The internal working name is `Charge`; the final UI name remains open. It must never be duplicated on both entities.

| Deployment state | Spatial bodies | Charge behavior | Intended trade |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Boarded (`Docked`) | Mech only | Strongest deliberate recharge and defensive conversion | Safety and recovery for reduced offensive access |
| Piloted / active | Mech only | Normal generation and spend | Main pressure state for Bulwark and Redline |
| Remote | Pilot + mech | Highest command/maintenance drain | Board coverage and flexibility, not double damage or two free turns |

Recommended rules for the first prototype:

1. **Boarding is not a free battery refill.** It enables a forecastable recharge action or capped end-of-activation gain.
2. **No infinite alternator loop.** Charge generated by an action cannot pay for that same action or recursively trigger more generation.
3. **Remote shares one command budget.** Pilot and mech may both exist spatially, but they do not each receive a full independent AP economy. Queued directives are the current recommendation until the prototype disproves them.
4. **Do not split incoming damage merely because two bodies exist.** Each target resolves its own Guard and Integrity. Remote is balanced through command bandwidth, Charge cost, exposure and opportunity cost.
5. **Full-charge discharge is an ability or capstone, not a passive talent.** Its new animation, VFX, audio and forecast treatment must be budgeted explicitly.

The exact pool size, generation rates and costs are intentionally absent. They are balance inputs, not architectural truths.

## 4. Specialty expression

### 4.1 Bulwark — boarded fortress

- Defaults to the pilot inside the mech.
- Boarding may convert a bounded amount of Charge into Guard or a defensive field.
- Defensive zones occupy square cells and preview exactly who receives a buff or debuff.
- Ordinary offensive projections are restricted while braced/charging.
- A branch talent may permit one reduced-output counter or construct strike while braced; it may not create a second full turn.
- The pilot may disembark to off-tank, intercept or solve a spatial problem, but loses the boarded bonuses.

### 4.2 Remote Arsenal — divided presence

- Pilot and mech are both spatial and vulnerable.
- The pilot uses a heavy projected rifle while the mech follows queued directives.
- Separation buys angles, elevation access and lane coverage.
- Charge, signal range, directive latency and exposed-pilot risk pay for that flexibility.
- The advanced “Mech Synth” fantasy becomes a synthetic-derived retrofit to the existing chassis: control tissue/skin, interface layer and actuators. It does not replace the mech's identity.

### 4.3 Redline — efficient bruiser

- Defaults to the pilot inside the stripped configuration.
- Melee, collision and fists/arms emphasize low projection cost.
- An alternator-like rule may regain a capped amount of Charge after qualifying committed actions.
- The gain resolves after the action and cannot chain with itself.
- Recovery still eventually requires giving up pressure through Board/Disengage behavior; the alternator delays the stop but does not eliminate it.
- Disembarking keeps the cyborg personally dangerous, but should be situational rather than the optimal permanent state.

## 5. Weapon chips and glove emitters

A chip is the fiction and visual form factor of the cyborg's character-specific `WeaponDefinition`, not a fifth gear piece or a second equipment taxonomy. Her gloves are emitters; the equipped program determines the projected combat form.

| Configuration | Projection | Reusable motion family |
|---|---|---|
| Bulwark | Defensive plane plus one heavy melee construct (mace **or** sword for the first slice) | guard projection + heavy strike |
| Remote Arsenal | Oversized heavy field rifle with deliberately bold OVA proportions | heavy aim + recoil shot |
| Redline | No visible projected weapon; calibrated mechanical-arm strikes | punch + committed impact |

Redline still carries an internal weapon record such as an unarmed calibration profile. “No visible chip weapon” must not become “no weapon slot” in data.

The slice should choose mace or sword, not author both immediately. A talent normally modifies an existing behavior; it cannot silently require a new weapon model and motion family.

## 6. Resource identities across the trio

Do not expose a generic `Mana` label. Reuse a common deterministic resource interface in code while giving each protagonist an economy that reinforces her verb.

| Character | Working direction | Earn/spend fantasy | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Human | Leverage / Momentum | observes mistakes, creates openings, cashes them into movement and handoffs | Name and existence not canon |
| Cyborg + mech | Charge / Drive | converts deployment, pressure and recovery into mechanical output | This document's prototype target |
| Synthetic | Conduction / Bandwidth | establishes links and spends access across units, machines and terrain | Name and existence not canon |

Shared team Synchronization remains separate and is earned through genuine cooperation.

## 7. First falsifying experiment

After the Unity bootstrap compiles, implement this first as engine-free scenario data and text forecasts. Do not model three visual configurations yet.

Run the same representative encounter with Bulwark, Remote Arsenal and Redline policies. Instrument:

- AP/command budget used by pilot and mech;
- Charge generated, spent, wasted and remaining per activation;
- damage, Guard and prevented damage by source;
- number and timing of Board/Deploy transitions;
- cells threatened and occupied;
- forecast/result parity for every resource and deployment transition;
- stalled turns and any renewable loop.

Provisional pass criteria:

1. Remote never produces two unrestricted full activations from one protagonist slot.
2. Every resource delta and occupancy consequence is shown before commitment and matches resolution exactly.
3. No action funds itself and no repeatable sequence produces unbounded Charge, Guard or damage.
4. Each Specialty has at least one representative turn where changing deployment is preferable and one where remaining in state is preferable.
5. A player can explain why boarding, deploying or staying put is the better choice without reading source code.

If a Specialty is strongest while ignoring deployment and Charge, the system has failed even if it is numerically balanced.

## 8. Main risks

| Risk | Why it matters | Cheapest serious check |
|---|---|---|
| Three configurations become three characters' worth of art | Destroys the small-team production budget | Modular-overlay breakdown using one skeleton before modelling |
| Remote becomes a free fourth protagonist | Invalidates encounter balance | Shared-budget A/B against single-body modes |
| Board/recharge creates a safe stall loop | Encourages passive play | Adversarial replay searching for renewable no-risk cycles |
| Defensive zones make Bulwark mandatory | Erases build choice | Same encounter with all three policies and Piercing/Area counters |
| Projected weapons silently triple animation scope | Talents become content explosions | Motion-archetype census before approving each construct |
| Synthetic retrofit erases the original mech | Breaks canon and emotional continuity | Silhouette overlay gate with core landmarks locked |

## 9. Decisions still required

1. Final player-facing name for the cyborg/mech resource.
2. Whether Bulwark's first offensive construct is a mace or sword.
3. Exact Board/Deploy AP and Charge costs.
4. Whether Remote uses queued directives, shared AP or another bounded command model after A/B testing.
5. Which Redline actions qualify for alternator recovery and its per-activation cap.
6. How much of the Synth-link retrofit is visible without changing the mech's canonical identity.

None of these questions blocks the current Unity bootstrap. They block implementation of this resource layer and production of Specialty-specific mech geometry.

## 10. Milestone 020 implementation note

The reusable state and resolver primitives are now implemented as a falsifying experiment without answering the open balance questions above. The values `3/8`, `+3`, `-2` and `1 AP` are scenario inputs only. They must not be copied into canon or authored content until play evidence supports them.

The experiment also makes one provisional command-budget choice concrete: in Remote mode the mech remains spatial, selectable and targetable but has no independent initiative slot. This prevents a free fourth protagonist while leaving queued directives for the next experiment. It is evidence-producing scaffolding, not approval of the final Remote command model.
